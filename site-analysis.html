Business Booster website audit

A full read of the current site before the rebuild: what it says, how it is built, how it performs for search and AI answers, and what the new version should keep, cut and add.

Audited 17 Nov 202629 pages crawledDesktop 1280px + mobile 375px

The short version

The technical foundation is better than most agency sites in Sri Lanka: clean metadata on every page, a sitemap, llms.txt for AI search, dark mode, lazy-loaded WebP images and a no-JavaScript fallback. The problems are in the homepage itself. It is 26 phone screens long, it talks in abstract terms ("commercial progress", "Growth OS", "digital staff") instead of showing results, and it carries almost no proof that the work pays off. The code has also grown in layers: the homepage ships 236 KB of CSS, about a third of which repeats earlier versions of the same styles.

Recommendation: keep the subpage structure, the SEO/AEO groundwork and the WhatsApp-first contact model. Rebuild the homepage from scratch around proof (results, client names, testimonials, real work) at roughly a third of its current length, and rebuild the stylesheet as one shared file instead of patching the existing one.

What is on the site

The site is a static build on GitHub Pages with no custom domain. The homepage links to 13 main pages; the sitemap adds nine more (eight system detail pages and a hotel marketing page that is only linked from Contact). Six client websites are hosted as folders on the same domain.

Messaging and content

Critical

The hero sells one service; the agency sells six

The H1 is "Is your social media growing your business?", and the About heading repeats it ("Is your social media a revenue stream for your business?"). The nav, meanwhile, lists social media, websites, search, ads and systems. A visitor who came for a website or a sales system has no reason to stay past the first screen.

lead with the outcome the whole agency delivers for Sri Lankan businesses, then route people by need (more enquiries, a new website, found on Google and AI, a system to run the business).

Critical

No proof that the work pays off

The only evidence on the homepage is "20+ active clients" (repeated four times), the founders' first names and a list of sectors. There are no client names next to results, no numbers, no testimonials, no case studies and no logos. Testimonials do appear, but only inside the hosted client sites. For an agency, this is the biggest conversion gap.

a results strip near the top (real metrics with client names), 3–4 short case studies (problem, what was done, what changed), a logo row and quoted testimonials with names and businesses.

Critical

Copy is abstract and repetitive

Most headings describe a philosophy rather than a service: "The difference is in the decisions", "Keep judgement near delivery", "Put the business in the middle", "Your digital staff should remember what people cannot". Phrases like "commercial progress" and "attention becomes action" recur throughout. A busy owner cannot tell from the homepage what they would get, how long it takes or roughly what it costs.

plain-language service descriptions with concrete deliverables ("12 reels and 20 posts a month", "5-page website in 3 weeks"), and starting prices or packages. llms.txt states "No prices are published on this site"; consider at least "from LKR X" ranges.

Important

A social media agency with no social links

The site links to no Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn or YouTube account of its own, and the JSON-LD schema has no sameAs. Homepage has no video, reel or photo work even though production is the lead service; old #video, #posts and #photography anchors now redirect to the website showcase.

a "Recent work" reel/post grid (embedded as images linking out), social icons in header/footer, and sameAs in schema.

Important

Developer notes are visible to visitors

The footer says "All company, video, post, photography, website, system and app content remains visible. The guided check and theme switching need JavaScript. WhatsApp links still work." The quiz also tells users "With JavaScript off, either answer opens a relevant WhatsApp conversation." This is build documentation, not customer copy.

remove these lines.

Important

Team is invisible

Founders appear as "Thulaib" and "Shiara" with no photos, surnames, roles or backgrounds. "One in-house team" is a key claim but no team is shown.

team photos with names and roles; founder story with faces.

Keep

Honest tone and status labels

Systems are labelled live, demo or in build; the quiz promises "No made-up score". This candour is a real differentiator worth keeping, just with more concrete content around it.

Design and user experience

Critical

The hero is hidden until a script animates it

Hero text, the nav and several blocks start at opacity:0 and wait for JavaScript. On the first mobile load the screen was entirely black apart from the small eyebrow line. Slow phones on mobile data will see an empty page for longer, and Google may measure a late Largest Contentful Paint.

render the hero fully visible in HTML/CSS; animate from a visible state only.

Critical

Two giant black screens before any substance

The hero and the quiz section are both full-height black panels with oversized type (the H1 is ~55px on a phone and doesn't fit the first desktop viewport at 1280×860). Visitors scroll two screens before learning what the agency does.

a compact hero: one clear line, one supporting line, primary CTA, and proof visible in the first screen.

Important

Quiz placed before trust is built

The "honest read" quiz is the second section. Asking people to answer questions before they know who you are is a big ask. It also duplicates /check-up/ and /rank/.

move the quiz to its own page (merge with check-up) and link to it from a mid-page block.

Important

Too many competing CTAs

Twelve WhatsApp CTAs with different wording ("Get my honest read", "Ask what to fix first", "Tell us your sector", "Show us the bottleneck", "Add this to my Growth OS"…). Each one is well meant, but together they dilute the main action. There's no form for people who prefer not to use WhatsApp.

one primary CTA used consistently, one secondary (e.g. book a call), plus a short form on Contact. Keep the context-specific pre-filled messages; they're useful for tracking where leads came from.

Important

Accessibility details

The faint text colour (#8c8c92 on #f5f5f2) is about 3.1:1 contrast, below the 4.5:1 needed for small text, and it's used for 11px eyebrows. 64 text elements render below 12px. Several nav and footer links are under 44px tap height on mobile.

minimum 13–14px for labels, 4.5:1 contrast, 44px tap targets.

Keep

What works

  • Light/dark theme with a toggle and system default
  • Skip link, semantic sections, alt text on every image
  • Reduced-motion styles throughout
  • No horizontal scrolling at phone width
  • Laptop mock-up showcase of a real site is a good idea; it just loads blank until the image arrives

Important

Stylesheet built in layers

Five <style> blocks (one is 181 KB) hold 2,337 rules, 766 of which redefine a selector already defined earlier, plus 74 !important overrides. Class names like hero-v4 and revenue-hero show each redesign was added on top of the previous one. Only ~32 KB of the homepage is actual content markup. The CSS is inlined per page, so browsers can't cache it between pages.

one shared, cached stylesheet with design tokens and components; start fresh rather than refactoring.

Important

Heavy client pages

The Scribblings of a Medic page is 415 KB because four images are embedded as base64 inside the HTML.

serve images as separate WebP files.

Keep

Lean delivery

Compressed transfer is about 63 KB with a single extra request on load. System fonts, no frameworks, no third-party scripts. Images are WebP with width/height set, lazy loading and srcset. Page loaded in under 2 seconds on desktop.

SEO, AEO and GEO

Critical

No custom domain

Every canonical URL points at businessboosterlk.github.io. Any authority the site builds belongs to a GitHub subdomain, and it reads as less established to clients. Moving later means redirects and lost ranking.

launch the new site on its own domain (e.g. businessbooster.lk) from day one.

Important

Portfolio sites are copies on the agency domain

The homepage says "Every project links directly to the shipped website", but all six links point to folders on businessboosterlk.github.io, not the clients' own domains. Indexing is inconsistent: Clove Beach is noindex, Sastho is noindex,nofollow with a canonical to /index.html, Sapphire Trails has no canonical, and the other three are indexable. Indexable copies can compete with the clients' real sites in Google.

link to live client domains where they exist; mark hosted demos noindex consistently; build proper case-study pages on the agency site instead.

Important

No analytics or conversion tracking

No Google Analytics, Tag Manager or Meta Pixel on the homepage. The Ads page promises "tracking set up before the first rupee is spent", but the agency's own site can't report which page or CTA produces enquiries. The privacy page frames this as a choice ("almost nothing" is kept).

privacy-friendly analytics (e.g. GA4 with consent, or Plausible) plus WhatsApp-click events, and an updated privacy page.

Improve

Schema can say more

The JSON-LD ProfessionalService has name, phone, email and locality only. Missing: logo, image, sameAs social profiles, founders, opening hours, geo coordinates, services offered and reviews. Service pages could add Service and FAQPage schema to support the AEO pitch.

Keep

Strong groundwork

  • Unique title, meta description, canonical, Open Graph and JSON-LD on every agency page
  • One H1 per page; descriptions 134–159 characters
  • robots.txt, XML sitemap and an llms.txt written for AI assistants
  • Local service pages written around Sri Lankan search terms (Colombo, Wadduwa, Nugegoda)
  • Old anchor links redirect to their new homes

Redesign roadmap

  1. Move to a custom domain

    Set up the domain first so the new site and all its content build authority in one place. Redirect github.io URLs.

  2. Gather proof before designing

    Collect 4–6 case studies with real numbers, client logos, 6+ named testimonials, team photos and the best reels/posts. The new homepage depends on this content.

  3. Rewrite the homepage around five screens

    Hero with clear offer and proof → results and logos → services by need → selected work and case studies → team and contact. Aim for 6–8 phone screens, not 26.

  4. Simplify the calls to action

    One primary WhatsApp CTA used everywhere, a "book a call" secondary, a short contact form. Keep per-page pre-filled WhatsApp text.

  5. Rebuild the front end cleanly

    A shared design-token stylesheet, visible-by-default content, 14px+ labels, AA contrast, 44px tap targets. Keep dark mode, reduced motion and the no-JS fallback.

  6. Turn the portfolio into case studies

    One page per client on the agency site; link out to the live client domain; noindex any hosted demos.

  7. Add measurement

    Analytics with consent, CTA click events per section, and a monthly look at which pages bring enquiries.

  8. Carry over the SEO work

    Keep URL paths for service pages, expand schema, add FAQ blocks, keep llms.txt current, and add images to service pages.
